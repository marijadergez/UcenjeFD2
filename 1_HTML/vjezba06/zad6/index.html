Kremasta Carbonara

Sastojci

  • 400g Špageta
  • 150g Guancialea
  • 4 Svježa jaja
  • 50g Pecorino Romano
  • puno Crnog papra

Priprema

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water according to package instructions.
  2. In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add cherry tomatoes and cook until they soften.
  4. Drain the pasta and add it to the pan with the tomatoes.
  5. Toss everything together and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve topped with grated parmesan cheese.
