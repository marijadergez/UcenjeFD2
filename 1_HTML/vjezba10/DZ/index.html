FPTV Akademija

FPV Academy refers to several educational programs and institutions specializing in FPV (First Person View) drone piloting, training, and technology. These academies cater to beginners, hobbyists, and professionals, offering structured courses in drone assembly, flight simulation, and real-world piloting. DroneFix.lt FPV College – A structured training system in Vilnius, Lithuania, developed in collaboration with Vilnius University’s Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics. It offers a 15–17 hour program covering FPV drone theory, simulator practice, soldering, drone assembly (using kits like "whoop" or 5–7 inch models), and flight. Participants receive a drone kit and can choose from 4 options, including a complete FPV system. Courses are for ages 16+, with group and private session options. Cost: €350–700+ depending on kit; individual sessions from €400. Next group: September 22–24, 2025 (in English). BL_CK Drone Academy – Offers intensive DJI and FPV drone training in Vilnius with professional instructors. Includes FPV drone basics (€149+), hands-on flight practice, and access to high-performance drones. Focuses on practical skills and real-world application. Features: One-day intensive courses, professional gear, and personalized coaching. Professional Drone Pilot School (DTS) – Located in Lithuania, this academy trains pilots with real-world battlefield experience from Ukraine’s Boryviter military school. Courses include 12 hours theory, 18 hours assembly, and 32 hours practical flying (62 total hours). Trained over 200 pilots from military and civilian sectors. Unique advantage: Instructors bring combat-tested knowledge directly to students. Code Academy Kids – Dronų būrelis – A youth-focused FPV program for 12–14-year-olds, combining STEM education with drone construction, flight simulation, and safety rules. Emphasizes problem-solving, diagnostics, and responsible drone use. Duration: 9 months; includes free first session. Other Notable Programs: DRONE VOLT Academy (France) – Offers professional training on DJI and custom drones (e.g., Hercules series) with real-world payload testing. OneDrone Academy (Slovenia) – Provides A1/A3, STS, and SORA certification courses based on current regulations and DJI equipment. FAUST FPV Academy (Croatia) – A STEM-focused project in Split, supported by CARNET, promoting digital inclusion and technology education in schools. These academies provide hands-on, practical training, often with real equipment, and are ideal for anyone seeking to enter FPV drone piloting, whether as a hobby, career, or educational pursuit.
