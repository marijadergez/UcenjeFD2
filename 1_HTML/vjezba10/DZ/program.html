FPV Academy refers to several educational programs and institutions specializing in FPV (First Person View) drone piloting, training, and technology. These academies cater to beginners, hobbyists, and professionals, offering structured courses in drone assembly, flight simulation, and real-world piloting. DroneFix.lt FPV College – A structured training system in Vilnius, Lithuania, developed in collaboration with Vilnius University’s Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics. It offers a 15–17 hour program covering FPV drone theory, simulator practice, soldering, drone assembly (using kits like "whoop" or 5–7 inch models), and flight. Participants receive a drone kit and can choose from 4 options, including a complete FPV system. Courses are for ages 16+, with group and private session options. Cost: €350–700+ depending on kit; individual sessions from €400. Next group: September 22–24, 2025 (in English). BL_CK Drone Academy – Offers intensive DJI and FPV drone training in Vilnius with professional instructors. Includes FPV drone basics (€149+), hands-on flight practice, and access to high-performance drones. Focuses on practical skills and real-world application.
